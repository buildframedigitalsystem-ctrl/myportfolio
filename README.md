# BuildFrame Portfolio

BuildFrame is a Website, Funnel, Platform & Software Builder focused on creating structured digital systems for businesses, organizations, professionals, services, and communities.

This portfolio showcases:

- Websites
- Funnels
- Booking Systems
- CRM & Office Systems
- Store Operating Systems
- Automation Workflows
- Google Sheets + Apps Script Integrations
- Membership & Community Platforms
- Business Dashboards
- Custom Business Platforms

## Core Vision

BuildFrame focuses on creating real operational systems — not just designs.

The goal is to help businesses simplify operations, automate processes, improve customer experience, and prepare for future expansion using practical and scalable digital structures.
